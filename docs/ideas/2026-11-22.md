## 1. Pick up the music where you left off (already exists)

**Correction, same evening:** AARE already has this. "Resume music" (`resume_media`, off by
default, old phone app › Connection; `CarRuntime.resumeMedia`) starts the last music app when
the car connects. The owner can simply turn it on. The variant below (only if music was playing
when the last drive ended, on by default) would change an existing setting's default and meaning,
so it is the owner's decision and was not built.

**Pitch:** if music was playing when the last drive ended, it starts again by itself when the
phone next connects to the car.

- **Why it fits:** Google's Android Auto, the owner's daily driver, does this, so MyPlay feels
  broken without it. Today `pauseIfFeedingCar` (`car/SessionEndMedia.kt`) pauses at the end of a
  drive, and nothing ever presses play again. With the owner's setup, sound goes over the car's
  own Bluetooth, so that function may not even run (it only pauses when the car's media stream
  is in use), and the player simply stops when the Bluetooth drops.
- **How:** at the end of a session, store whether music was playing and which app was playing it
  (`myplay_resume_music_pkg`, not backed up). On the next connection, once the media session is
  there, send play to that app only (`MediaHub`), and only if the drive ended less than 12 h ago.
  The setting `myplay_resume_music` is on by default and can be turned off. The gesture line
  shows "Playing: <title>", so it is clear why music started.
- **Size:** S.
- **Files:** `car/SessionEndMedia.kt`, `car/CarSession.kt` (session end around line 1280, and
  session start), a new `car/MyPlayResume.kt` (setting, rule, tests), `MyPlayBackupKeys.kt`
  (the on/off setting is backed up, the stored app is not).
- **Conflicts:** none with the button hints. In the phone app rebuild the switch's row belongs in
  Car › Sound (chunk 7). Until then the setting is on and has no row, as other car-side settings
  have been.
- **Unknown:** the Audi may itself send play when its Bluetooth reconnects. Pressing play twice
  does no harm, but the owner should say if music starts twice or skips.

## 2. Ice warning on the clock disc (TONIGHT)

**Pitch:** a small snowflake next to the clock, and once per drive a gesture line saying
"3 °C outside: roads may be icy", when the car reports 4 °C or less.

- **Why it fits:** it is late November in Lithuania. The Audi's own cluster does the same.
  `CarData.State.environment.temperatureC` already exists and ORBIT never shows it.
- **How:** a pure function `iceRisk(tempC) = tempC <= 4f` (4 °C is the usual car-maker
  threshold), plus a small icon in ORBIT's top-right status disc. With no temperature from the
  car, nothing is drawn, so the screen stays exactly as it is.
- **Size:** S.
- **Files:** a new `ui/car/myplay/MyPlayIce.kt` (the rule and its tests), the top-right
  clock/status part in `ui/car/myplay/orbit/OrbitSkin.kt`, `ui/car/GestureLine.kt` (one more
  `Words` value).
- **Conflicts:** the button hints also change `OrbitSkin.kt`, but they draw around the speed
  circle, and this is the clock disc. Small merge risk only; fetch and merge before pushing.
  The phone app is not touched.
- **Unknown:** whether the box sends the outside temperature at all. The Car check log will say
  (look for the environment sensor). If it does not, this shows nothing and costs nothing.

## 3. One-line drive summary in the connection log (TONIGHT)

**Pitch:** when a drive ends, one log line says how long it was, how far, which speed source was
used, and what share of the time the speed limit was known, unsure or missing.

- **Why it fits:** the speed limit is "very important" to the owner, but nobody can tell yet
  whether the Lithuania file covers their roads. Today that takes reading hundreds of lines.
  This line answers it every drive, and is the base for idea 12.
- **How:** `SpeedFeed` (`car/MyPlaySpeed.kt`) already sees every reading and every limit answer.
  A small counter adds up the seconds per state, and on session end writes `drive: 23 min,
  17.4 km (speed: car 92 %, phone 8 %); limit known 71 %, unsure 9 %, none 20 %`. Distance comes
  from the speed readings, and from the odometer if the box sends it. On purpose it does **not**
  log maximum speed or time over the limit: the owner shares this log with sessions.
- **Size:** S.
- **Files:** a new `car/DriveSummary.kt` (counter and tests), `car/MyPlaySpeed.kt` (feed the
  counter), `car/CarSession.kt` (write the line at session end), `DiagLog`.
- **Conflicts:** none. It is log only, with no UI.

## 4. "Phone is hot" and "phone battery low" notices (TONIGHT)

**Built without the battery half:** ORBIT already shows the battery in the warning colour at 20 %
or less, so a second warning would be noise.

**Pitch:** if the phone starts to overheat, or its battery falls under 15 % while not charging,
the gesture line says so once ("Phone is hot: it may slow down").

- **Why it fits:** wireless projection, encoding the video and Waze together heat a phone, and
  Android 16 slows a hot phone down. That shows up as a stuttering car screen, which looks like
  a MyPlay bug. Telling the owner why helps them and helps anyone reading reports. The battery
  level is already read (`car/PhoneStatusBridge.kt`).
- **How:** `PowerManager.addThermalStatusListener` (Android 10 and newer, no permission). At
  `THERMAL_STATUS_SEVERE` or worse, show one gesture line and write one log line. Do the same for
  battery under 15 % and not charging. Each notice at most once per drive. Nothing changes
  automatically (no lower resolution: that would be a decision).
- **Size:** S.
- **Files:** `car/PhoneStatusBridge.kt` (thermal listener next to the battery receiver), a new
  `car/PhoneHealth.kt` (rules and tests), `ui/car/GestureLine.kt` (two more `Words`).
- **Conflicts:** none. It does not touch `OrbitSkin.kt`.

## 5. Places: a Navigate list with Home, Work and favourites

**Pitch:** ORBIT's Navigate world opens a short list (Home, Work, up to six of your own), turn
and push to start Waze there.

- **Why it fits:** the button-hints job adds a "Home address" and a "Navigate home" action.
  This is the natural next step. Typing in Waze with a knob is painful, and the owner drives the
  same few places. `CarRuntime.navigateTo` already does the hand-off.
- **Size:** M.
- **Files:** a new `ui/car/myplay/MyPlayPlaces.kt` (stored list, `myplay_places`), ORBIT's
  Navigate world in `OrbitSkin.kt` and `MyPlayEngine.kt`, `host/CarRuntime.kt` (no change
  expected). Phone: a "Places" page under Car › Maps.
- **Conflicts:** **builds on the button-hints job**, so start after it lands and reuse its Home
  address key. It also needs a phone page, so it waits for the phone app's chunk 7 (Car II), or
  gets a slot added to §8 of the phone plan.

## 6. "Report to Waze" from the pad

**Pitch:** a pad direction or a learned button opens Waze's report menu (police, hazard,
traffic) directly, ready for the knob.

- **Why it fits:** the owner keeps Waze *because of* police and road reports. Reporting today
  means reaching Waze's own buttons with the ring.
- **How:** if Waze's car template shows a "Report" action in its action strip, a new
  `HomePadAction.REPORT` (and `ButtonAction.REPORT`) presses it through the hosted template.
  First a research step: does Waze's template expose it, under what name, and does pressing it
  work outside Waze's own screen?
- **Size:** M, after a small research step.
- **Files:** `ui/car/myplay/MyPlayHomePad.kt`, `car/ButtonTable.kt`, `host/` (the template's
  action strip), and the hints (an icon for the new action).
- **Conflicts:** the hints read the pad mapping live, so a new action needs a hint icon. Do it
  after the hints job. The phone app's action picker (chunk 6) has to list it.

## 7. The limit disc pulses when the limit changes

**Pitch:** when the speed limit changes (50 → 70), the limit ring gives one soft pulse, so the
eye catches it without a sound.

- **Why it fits:** the owner wants a "clear but calm" warning with no sound. A change of limit is
  the moment that matters, and today it simply swaps silently.
- **Size:** S.
- **Files:** the speed and limit part in `ui/car/myplay/orbit/OrbitSkin.kt` (and `OrbitParts.kt`
  for the animation), with a screenshot test of both frames.
- **Conflicts:** **yes, with the button hints:** they draw a ring of icons around the same speed
  circle. Do it after that job lands.

## 8. Quick replies, with "I'll be there at 14:32"

**Pitch:** under a message read aloud, a short list of replies (turn, push, sent). One of them
fills in Waze's arrival time.

- **Why it fits:** messages are already read aloud (`notify/MessagingScreen.kt`,
  `CarNotificationListener.kt`), but answering needs the keyboard and the car parked. With knob
  only, picking a ready reply is the only way to answer while driving. The arrival time is
  already in the turn data (`car/NavStatusBridge.kt`). The reply goes through the messaging
  app's own reply action, so nothing new leaves the phone except the message the owner chose
  to send.
- **Size:** M.
- **Files:** `notify/MessagingScreen.kt`, `notify/CarNotificationListener.kt` (RemoteInput),
  ORBIT's notification slot (`OrbitSkin.kt`, `MyPlayEngine.kt`), a new
  `notify/QuickReplies.kt`. Phone: editing the replies under Car › Messages.
- **Conflicts:** none with the hints. The phone page belongs to the phone app's chunk 7 (design
  frame 36 is Car › Messages). The car side can ship first with three built-in replies.

- **Why it fits:** it combines the owner's two loves, Waze's reports and the speed read-out, and
  it is the RACE "lap time" feel inside ORBIT.
- **Size:** L.
- **Files:** `ui/car/myplay/MyPlayAlerts.kt` (recognising the alert), `car/MyPlaySpeed.kt` (the
  average), the speed part in `OrbitSkin.kt`.
- **Conflicts:** the same circle as the hints (do it after them).
- **Waits for:** the owner's drive with "maps text" on (0.1.46 asked for it). Only that shows
  whether Waze tells us when a section starts and ends. Do not guess the shape.

## 10. Call back the last missed call with one push

**Pitch:** after a missed call, the Calls world opens with "Call back <name>" already ringed.

- **Why it fits:** it is the commonest call action in a car, and with the knob it should be one
  push, not a list walk. Call history is already read for the phone screen
  (`ui/car/PhoneScreen.kt`, `car/Calls.kt`).
- **Size:** S–M.
- **Files:** `car/Calls.kt`, ORBIT's calls world (`OrbitSkin.kt`, `MyPlayEngine.kt` focus start),
  and a focus test.
- **Conflicts:** none with the phone app. Check how far ORBIT's calls (N8) have got before
  starting.

## 11. Where I parked

**Pitch:** when the drive ends, the phone remembers where the car stopped. One tap on a quiet
notification opens it in a map.

- **Why it fits:** the phone already has a fresh position at the end of every drive (the speed
  fallback or the car's GPS feed). It only needs keeping. Stored only on the phone, never sent.
- **Size:** S–M.
- **Files:** a new `car/ParkedHere.kt`, `car/CarSession.kt` (session end), `car/PhoneLocationFeed.kt`
  (last fix), and a notification channel. Later a card on the new Home (phone chunk 2's
  attention cards).
- **Conflicts:** the Home card is the phone app worker's. The notification alone does not
  conflict.
- **Decision for the owner:** on or off by default, and whether a notification after every drive
  is welcome.

## 12. Drive card after you park

**Pitch:** after each drive, a small card on the phone: "23 min · 17.4 km · limit known 71 % of
the way", plus a weekly total.

- **Why it fits:** it is fun, it is the owner's own data and stays on the phone, and it builds

- **Size:** M.
- **Files:** idea 3's `car/DriveSummary.kt` (store the last drives), the new phone Home's attention

- **Conflicts:** **with the phone app rebuild:** it is a new kind of Home card, so the phone app
  worker does it, or §8 of the phone plan gets a slot first.
