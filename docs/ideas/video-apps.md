# Video apps on the car (YouTube, MovieBox Pro)

The real phone app runs on the car's own display (Phone apps, with root or Shizuku). The phone's
screen is never mirrored. Video plays only when parked.

- **Which YouTube:** the "Video app" chosen on the phone; else whatever opens YouTube links and is
  not a browser, a patched build (Morphe, ReVanced) before Google's; else an app named YouTube.
  MovieBox Pro is found by its name. No package is fixed.
- **One entry:** an app on the car as itself is not also listed as a media player (YouTube Music
  stays one). Tapping YouTube opens the real app.
- **Turned on once (VA-11):** the first time root or Shizuku is there, MyPlay turns **Phone apps on
  the car** on and adds the YouTube app and MovieBox Pro to it. Taking either off afterwards sticks.
- **Play on car (VA-10):** MyPlay in the share sheet. A shared link opens in the car's video app
  (only an app already allowed on the car, only while connected). Any app the owner shares from
  can send it a link this way; it opens in that video app on the car, never anything else. The
  phone's copy is paused. While driving the link waits and opens once parked.
- **Open on car:** the music panel's app stop opens a video app that is playing as the real app, at
  the same video when its session gives the video id.
- **On the car:** the app fills the screen sideways; leaving it (the map, the home) or driving keeps
  it 2 minutes, so it carries on; driving off pauses it (picture hidden, sound off).
- **Sound:** with root, MyPlay's audio takes the phone's output, so the app's sound reaches the car
  ("Root audio" on).
