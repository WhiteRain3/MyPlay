# Car check, 23 November 2026 (MyPlay 0.1.48 to 0.1.53)

Everything below shipped overnight and has only been tested on the computer, never in the car.
Each item: what to do, then what "good" looks like. Note anything that is not good (a photo
helps), then send the two logs at the end.

## Parked

1. **On the home, press Back (or touch the screen), then wait.**
   Good: small icons appear round the speed circle (up: Waze, left and right: the dial) and fade after a few seconds. No icon for a direction set to nothing. (A knob turn brings the knob's ring instead, and the pad icons hide while it shows.)
2. **Teach a corner button "Navigate home"** (phone: Buttons, Teach a button), type your home address in the field under it, then press the button.
   Good: Waze starts a route home (it may ask you to tap Go). Its icon now shows on the upper left of the ring.
3. **Clear the home address, press that button again.**
   Good: the car says "Set home address on the phone"; nothing else happens.
4. **Play a song with a long title, and one with wide album art.**
   Good: the art is always a perfect circle, never an oval; the long title is cut with "…".
5. **Look at the clock corner for a full minute.**
   Good: the clock, battery and temperature never shift sideways as the numbers change.
6. **Turn on "Show the map's free area" (phone: Behind the scenes) with Waze open.**
   Good: the outlined box is centred and nearly full height; Waze's arrow sits in its middle.
7. **With that box showing, look at the top left (MC-3).**
   Good: the turn's distance and road name do not hide a Waze button you need (its report or speed button).
8. **Wait for a Waze alert, or tap one (MC-4).**
   Good: the alert pill sits low in the middle, between the speed and the song, and does not cover Waze's own bottom bar or ETA.
9. **Set a home pad direction to Places** (phone: Buttons), save one place, press that direction.
   Good: a list of Home and your place; push one and Waze routes there.
10. **Teach a button "Call back"** after a missed call, press it.
    Good: it asks "Call back …?" and only calls when you push Call.
11. **Turn on "Remember where I parked", then disconnect the phone from the car.**
    Good: the phone shows where you parked, with a way back in a maps app.
12. **On the new phone app (Settings, About, tap the version 7 times), tap a MyPlay notification (NA-2).**
    Good: it opens the matching page (Home or Certificates), not a blank or wrong one.
13. **Turn the phone sideways and back while the phone screen is mirrored on the car (MS-1).**
    Good: the mirror comes back each time, no black or frozen picture.

## Driving (passenger, or a glance only)

14. **Drive past a speed limit change.**
    Good: the limit sign pulses once, softly, and shows the new number.
15. **Look by the clock (if the car reports the outside temperature).**
    Good: the temperature is always there; at 4 °C or less also a snowflake, and one short "road may be icy" line per drive.
16. **Push the knob twice quickly, then three times, while music plays.**
    Good: next song, then previous song; a single push still works, a little after you press.
17. **When a call rings, push the knob once.**
    Good: it answers at once, with no wait and no song skip.
18. **Watch the soft shades behind ORBIT's parts on a bright map (SH-2, SH-3).**
    Good: soft edges round the turn, song and clock, no hard boxes, and no stutter while they fade.
19. **When a notice with buttons and a Waze alert show at the same time.**
    Good: they never cover each other, and after both go the knob's highlight is back where it was.
20. **At the end of the drive, nothing to do.**
    Good: the drive summary line is in the log (minutes and kilometres only).
