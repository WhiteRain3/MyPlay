# Car check, 23 November 2026 (MyPlay 0.1.48 to 0.1.53)

Everything below shipped overnight and has only been tested on the computer, never in the car.
Each item: what to do, then what "good" looks like. Note anything that is not good (a photo
helps), then send the two logs at the end.

## Parked

1. **On the home, press Back (or touch the screen), then wait.**
   Good: small icons appear round the speed circle (from 0.1.57: up next song, right Waze, down home, left music) and fade after a few seconds. No icons on the diagonals any more. No icon for a direction set to nothing. (A knob turn brings the knob's ring instead, and the pad icons hide while it shows.)
2. **Type your home address in Car › Buttons, then press the pad down on the home** (from 0.1.57 the pad's down is Navigate home; a corner button that sends "down" does the same).
   Good: Waze starts a route home, with the knob's highlight already on Go now.
3. **Clear the home address, press the pad down again.**
   Good: the car says "Set home address on the phone"; nothing else happens.
4. **Play a song with a long title, and one with wide album art.**
   Good: the art is always a perfect circle, never an oval; the long title is cut with "…".
5. **Look at the clock corner for a full minute.**
   Good: the clock, battery and temperature never shift sideways as the numbers change.
6. **Turn on "Show the map's free area" (phone: More › Advanced › Developer) with Waze open.**
   Good: the outlined box is centred and nearly full height; Waze's arrow sits in its middle.
7. **With that box showing, look at the top left (MC-3), during a route with a lane choice.**
   Good: the turn's distance and road name do not hide a Waze button you need (its report or speed button). From 0.1.56: before a turn with lanes, a small pill under the turn arrow shows the lanes (the ones to take in green), stays inside the turn's corner and covers nothing of Waze's.
8. **Wait for a Waze alert, or tap one (MC-4).**
   Good: the alert pill sits low in the middle, between the speed and the song, and does not cover Waze's own bottom bar or ETA.
9. **Set a home pad direction to Places** (phone: Car › Buttons › ORBIT home: pad shortcuts), save one place in Car › Buttons, press that direction.
   Good: a list of Home and your place; push one and Waze routes there.
10. **Teach a button "Call back"** after a missed call, press it.
    Good: it asks "Call back …?" and only calls when you push Call.
11. **Turn on "Remember where I parked", then disconnect the phone from the car.**
    Good: the phone shows where you parked, with a way back in a maps app.
12. **On the new phone app (Settings, About, tap the version 7 times), tap a MyPlay notification (NA-2).**
    Good: it opens the matching page (Home or Certificates), not a blank or wrong one.
13. **Turn the phone sideways and back while the phone screen is mirrored on the car (MS-1).**
    Good: the mirror comes back each time, no black or frozen picture.

27. **On the home, push the knob, then try each pad direction (from 0.1.57).**
    Good: the dial opens with Apps in the middle; up opens Waze, right Calls, down Settings, left Music; pushing again opens Apps. Back from Calls, Settings or Music goes back to the dial; Back from the dial goes home. The pad's up on the home plays the next song.
28. **Reply to a message from a notice, or open any screen with one main button (GO-2).**
    Good: the knob's highlight starts on the main button (Send, Go now) on each new screen, not on Back; on a confirm screen, check it starts on the button you expect before pushing.
30. **Open Waze full screen from ORBIT (from 0.1.58), then push the knob.**
    Good: no buttons on Waze's map; the push opens a wheel of Waze's actions (then "Move map", then "MyPlay home"); turning moves the highlight; a push runs the row and closes the wheel. "Move map" lets the pad move the map and the knob zoom; a push or Back ends it.
31. **On Waze's map, press Back once, then wait (WH-2).**
    Good: Back shows the wheel; it does not leave Waze. Back again at once goes to MyPlay's home ("Back twice for home"). After turning in the wheel, Back only closes it.
32. **On Waze's map with a message notice showing (Reply), press Back.**
    Good: Back dismisses the notice; the wheel does not open.
33. **Pick a destination in Waze (route preview).**
    Good: on ORBIT, Go now is the big round button right of the routes (GD-2), its words inside the circle; the knob's highlight is on it, and one push starts the drive.
37. **A WhatsApp message arrives while driving (Reply and Mark as read) (NB-1).**
    Good: the banner sits right of the middle, clear of the turn and the clock; "Message · hidden while driving"; the buttons' words are readable at a glance, and the highlight is on Dismiss.
35. **Open Settings, Places, Car, Calls and the notifications list on ORBIT (from 0.1.59), and turn the knob.**
    Good: each list turns like a wheel, one row per click; the ringed row stays in the middle and biggest, with one faded row either side; the dot on the left arc and "N more" above and below follow. Long setting notes fit their row. A touch on a faded row only brings it to the middle; a second touch runs it.
36. **On Waze full screen (ORBIT), open its destinations list, then its settings (parked), and turn the knob (WL-1).**
    Good: the row the knob is on stays in the middle; the others fade and bend left, and long names at the edges are still readable enough to know what is there.
38. **Have someone call you while Waze is navigating (CB-2).**
    Good: the map stays bright; Answer is on the left with the highlight on it, Decline on the right; one push answers.
39. **During that call, look at the bottom of the screen, then wait for a Waze alert (CB-1, CB-2).**
    Good: the call pill sits at the bottom middle and hides nothing of Waze's own bottom controls (report button, arrival bar); when an alert shows, the pill moves to the top, and back down after.
40. **While Waze navigates on ORBIT's home, wait for a reroute, then arrive (NM-2).**
    Good: "Recalculating…" shows ORBIT's spinning ring in the turn's place, then "You have arrived" shows its icon and title, then the turn comes back.
41. **Open Waze full screen by day and by night (NM-2).**
    Good: Waze's own screens (lists, buttons, messages) use ORBIT's colours in both.

## Driving (passenger, or a glance only)

14. **Drive past a speed limit change.**
    Good: the limit sign pulses once, softly, and shows the new number.
29. **Brake to a stop, then speed up past a limit (SP-2, SP-3).**
    Good: the speed follows the car. If the car sends its speed only now and then, the number can stay at the car's last value for up to 10 seconds while braking; on the phone's GPS it is smoothed, so the over-limit colour can come a second late.
15. **Look by the clock (if the car reports the outside temperature).**
    Good: the temperature is always there; at 4 °C or less also a snowflake, and one short "road may be icy" line per drive.
16. **Push the knob twice quickly, then three times, while music plays.**
    Good: next song, then previous song; a single push still works, a little after you press.
17. **When a call rings, push the knob once.**
    Good: it answers at once, with no wait and no song skip.
18. **Watch the soft shades behind ORBIT's parts on a bright map (SH-2, SH-3).**
    Good: soft edges round the turn, song and clock, no hard boxes, and no stutter while they fade.
19. **When a notice with buttons and a Waze alert show at the same time.**
    Good: they never cover each other, and after both go the knob's highlight is back where it was.
20. **At the end of the drive, nothing to do.**
    Good: the drive summary line is in the log (minutes and kilometres only).

## Phone app (from 0.1.57: the new phone app is the only one)

21. **Open MyPlay on the phone.**
    Good: the new app with its five tabs (Home, Screen, Car, Apps, More); nothing leads back to the old screens.
22. **Car tab: "Sound to the car".**
    Good: the switch is on the Car tab, with "Also the maps app's voice" under it. The rest of Sound is in More › Advanced › Sound. Waze's voice still comes through the car as before.
23. **Apps › Phone apps on this (rooted) phone.**
    Good: before root is allowed, one card "This phone is rooted" with **Allow root** (the su prompt opens); after allowing, "Using root" and no Shizuku steps. On a phone without root you would see three Shizuku steps (install, Start, Allow) with the current one marked, and "How to set up" opening the help page.
25. **Car › Voice assistant, then a car app's voice search** (Waze's microphone button).
    Good: if the microphone isn't allowed, Car shows "Microphone not allowed" and Voice shows "Allow the microphone"; once allowed, voice search hears you.
26. **Once, on a fresh install (or after clearing MyPlay's data, which loses its settings: save a backup first).**
    Good: Setup opens (four steps, no tab bar), not What's new; each permission shows "Allowed" once granted; a MyPlay notification (an update, a car to trust) opens its page in the app.
24. **Screen › Looks: pick a look while on DECK** (More › Archive), then while on ORBIT.
    Good: the design stays as it was each time; only colours, wallpaper and layout follow the look.

34. **Home › Preview (without the car).**
    Good: full screen, landscape; "Hide controls" / "Testing controls" toggles the bar; Push held, pushed twice and three times does what the knob does in the car; the knob turns move the ring; a pretend speed shows its number on ORBIT; the phone's Back walks back and only leaves from the dashboard. A teach armed on Car › Buttons is cancelled when the Preview opens.
