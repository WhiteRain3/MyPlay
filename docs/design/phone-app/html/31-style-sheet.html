  • 4 dp grid. Side gutter 16. Gap between cards 12. Section head 20 above, 8 below.
  • Touch target ≥ 48 × 48 everywhere; rows 56–64 (72 with help).
  • Radii: card 24 · picture 20 · sheet 28 · buttons and tags fully round · icon disc 40 circle.
  • Tab bar 72 + gesture area 24. Top bar 72 (root) / 64 (page).

Motion

  • Pages: shared axis X, 300 ms, emphasized easing (cubic-bezier(.2,0,0,1)); Back runs it in reverse and follows the predictive-back gesture.
  • Tabs: fade through, 200 ms; each tab keeps its stack and scroll.
  • Sheets: rise 280 ms, scrim fades to 66 %; drag down or Back closes.
  • Rings: progress sweeps clockwise from 12 o'clock; waiting rings breathe (scale 1 → 1.06, opacity .25 → 0, 1.6 s loop); connected ring draws once, 600 ms.
  • Switches: thumb 150 ms + light haptic. Reorder lift: 8 dp shadow + accent edge + haptic.
  • Changes: the live car picture cross-fades 200 ms on every setting change, so the change is seen, not explained.
  • Respect "Remove animations": every motion falls back to an instant cut or a 100 ms fade.
