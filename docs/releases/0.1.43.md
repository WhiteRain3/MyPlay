**ORBIT is now MyPlay's car screen, and an information-only home**

- **Every install now uses ORBIT, MyPlay's own car screen, after this update**, whatever was
  chosen before (it was a preview until now). To go back to an older layout (taskbar, dock,
  classic, map behind), turn off the ORBIT switch on the phone's Layout page (it may still be called "Try
  MyPlay's own car screen (preview)"; a new name is coming); that
  choice is kept. The older layouts will be removed later. If you restore a settings backup on
  a new phone, choose the older layout again once. DECK is still there to choose.
- **Information-only home (optional, off by default):** ORBIT's home without the knob's
  highlight at all: the pad shortcuts and the buttons you taught do everything. Switch it on in
  the car's Settings ("Home: information only (no ring)") or on the phone. It only takes effect
  while a pad direction opens the dial or Apps (the defaults do), so the car's Settings are
  always reachable from the car (the phone's switch does not say so yet). A notice or a call still brings the highlight for its buttons.
- **Teaching a car button** now names the action on the car screen ("Open the maps app"), each
  time you start; the phone page stops waiting by itself after 30 s, and the pad shortcut hint
  now says correctly that "nothing" only shows the highlight.

Still wanted: the Car check log (the knob, the pad and the 4 buttons round the knob), and a
drive's log with the map-area check on, so Waze's arrow can sit better in ORBIT's free middle.

It builds and passes its tests, but nothing in it has been tried in the car.

Host shims: unchanged since 0.1.4 (no need to reinstall).
