**ORBIT: the pad opens things, a four-way dial, no doubled turn card**

- **The pad opens things directly on ORBIT's home.** Push the knob: the music panel (the Music
  page when nothing plays). Pad up: Waze (your maps app) full screen. Pad left or right: the dial.
  The knob's highlight is hidden until you turn the knob; then the pad and the push work on the
  highlighted part as before, until it hides again after a few seconds. Change what each
  direction does on the phone: Buttons → "ORBIT home: pad shortcuts" (a direction set to
  "nothing" only shows the highlight).
- **The dial has four worlds, one per pad direction:** up Calls, right Apps, down Settings, left
  Car. One press of a direction opens it; turning and pushing still work. The line of help under
  the dial is gone.
- **No doubled turn card on the home:** Waze's turn card (drawn by MyPlay from what Waze sends) is
  left out while ORBIT shows the next turn itself; it comes back whenever ORBIT has none.
- **Waze full screen can go home by itself** after 1, 2, 5 or 10 minutes untouched: off by
  default, in the car's Settings ("Maps full screen: home when untouched").
- **The line under the speed** in the hub is now one larger value: the time left.

**Please:** turn off Waze's own speedometer, now that MyPlay shows the speed and the limit:
Waze → Settings → Speedometer → "Show on map" off. MyPlay cannot hide it (Waze draws it on its map).

- **Teach the car's buttons (the 4 round the knob) an action.** On the phone: Buttons → "Teach a
  car button", choose what it should do (open Waze, open music, home or apps…), then press the
  button in the car within 30 s; the car screen says when it is waiting. Back, the knob, the pad
  and the call buttons cannot be taught, so they always keep working. If the phone page still
  says it is waiting after 30 s, tap Cancel and try again (a fix is coming).

Known: the phone's pad shortcut hint says a direction set to "nothing" moves the highlight; it
only shows it (fixed in the next release).

Please also run the Car check and press the knob, the pad and those 4 buttons, and share the log.

It builds and passes its tests, but nothing in it has been tried in the car. DECK is unchanged.

Host shims: unchanged since 0.1.4 (no need to reinstall).
