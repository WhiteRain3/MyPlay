**ORBIT: the speed limit as a number, no darkening when it is not needed**

- **The speed and the speed limit are in the round hub** (bottom left), with the arrival time
  and time left under the speed. The limit is shown as a road-sign ring with its number on the
  hub's edge: red with the number when the map data knows it, grey with a dash when it does
  not. The speed turns amber when you are over the limit by your margin, as before. The
  separate speed circle under the clock is gone.
- **No darkening when nothing is there:** the dark shade in the top-left corner shows only while
  there is a route (behind the next turn), and the one in the bottom-right only while music
  plays. Without navigation or music the map is clear.
- **Apps open more smoothly:** an app (the map included) now appears through a window that grows
  from what you picked, instead of being scaled, so a map should no longer flicker as it opens.

The speed limit only shows with the speed source on Auto, a region chosen and its file
downloaded on the phone (MyPlay, Speed); if it never shows, the connection log says why. The
time and time left under the speed may be small on your screen: say if it is hard to read.

It builds and passes its tests, but nothing in it has been tried in the car. DECK is unchanged.

Host shims: unchanged since 0.1.4 (no need to reinstall).
