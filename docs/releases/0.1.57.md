**Your pad and dial, your colours on ORBIT, the car's own speed, and the new phone app**

- **The pad on ORBIT's home, as you asked:** up plays the next song, right opens Waze, down
  navigates home (set the address in Car › Buttons; without one the car says so), left opens the
  music, and a push opens the dial. A mapping you already changed on the phone stays as it is.
- **The dial is four ways now:** up Waze, right Calls, down Settings, left Music; push again for
  Apps. Back from a world goes back to the dial. Car data is no longer on the dial; for now it
  opens only from a car-data app tile, and the next update puts it first in Settings.
- **No more icons on the diagonals:** your corner buttons send the pad's up and down, so their
  icons are the pad's own.
- **Your theme, accent and colours now colour ORBIT** (phone: Screen › Colours, and Looks): the
  glass behind ORBIT's parts, its text, and the main colour (the turn, the knob's highlight) follow
  what you choose, at once, on the car. (The phone's picture of the car screen still shows
  ORBIT's own colours; that comes in the next update.) Keep the default theme and
  its own accent to have ORBIT's own look. Picking a look no longer changes the design.
- **Control size makes ORBIT's parts bigger** (phone: Screen › Size), without changing the text
  size.
- **The speed comes from the car when the car sends it:** many cars send their speed only when it
  changes, and MyPlay used to fall back to the phone's GPS between updates. The car's speed now
  holds for 10 seconds (so while braking it can stay a little high for up to that long); the
  phone's GPS is used only after that, and is smoothed (so the over-limit colour can come a second
  late). The phone's picture shows CAR or GPS on the speed circle, and the connection log counts
  the switches.
- **"Go now" in one push:** when Waze shows the route after you pick a destination, the knob's
  highlight is already on Go now. Any new screen with one main button starts on it.
- **Smoother ORBIT:** the corner shades, opening an app, the song's clock, the dial and the
  wallpaper do less work on each frame.
- **Browser, Car data and phone apps on the car** switch on and off at once, without
  reconnecting.
- **The new phone app is the only one:** five tabs (Home, Screen, Car, Apps, More). Phone apps
  on a rooted phone ask for root first, before any Shizuku steps; below Android 12 the Shizuku card says it needs Android 12. Car › Voice asks for the
  microphone again when it is missing.

It builds and passes its tests, but nothing in it has been tried in the car.

Host shims: unchanged since 0.1.4 (no need to reinstall).
