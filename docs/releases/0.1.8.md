**On the phone**

- Two new pages for the car screen's top corner, opened by tapping the clock or the status icons
  in the car picture (before, both opened the Bar page):
  - **Status icons**: show the battery as an icon, a number, or both, with or without the %
    sign; show or hide the signal and Wi-Fi; four sizes; a colour of your choice; and "colour by
    level" (red up to 15 %, amber up to 30 %, green above).
  - **Clock**: four sizes, bold or not, and a colour. The Retro look keeps its own digits but
    takes the size and colour.
- The car picture on the phone now shows the status icons and clock in the look you chose.
- The status strip on these pages fits narrow phones.

**On the car screen**

- A larger clock no longer gets cut off in a side bar: it shrinks to fit the bar's width. So in
  a side bar, Large and Larger may look no bigger than Normal; they show fully in a bottom bar.
- At the larger sizes, the side bar gives up a recent app, the status icons or Settings sooner
  on short screens, so everything still fits.
- With the battery icon and number both switched off, nothing is left where the battery was
  (the charging bolt still shows while charging).

Nothing in this release has been tried in the car yet.
