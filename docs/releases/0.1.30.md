**With the knob and the buttons**

- **The music card** (Map behind): a push of the knob now always moves the ring to the card's
  buttons on the first press. Before, it sometimes stayed on the card and needed a second press.
- **A short line after a button gesture:** when a button changes the song, plays or pauses, or
  starts the assistant, a line shows at the top of the car screen for a moment: "Next song:
  <title>", "Paused: <title>", "Assistant". It waits for the new song's title rather than showing
  the old one, sits under a notice rather than on top of it, and stays away while a call rings.
  A switch on the phone turns it off.
- **Calls:** Back declines a ringing call, and a Decline button shows, only when MyPlay is allowed
  to end calls (the phone's permission for it). Without that permission Back does its usual job.
- **Music:** quick presses left or right on the progress bar now add up (two presses: 20 s).
- **Moving a map with the knob:** if you had turned on the maps app's own pan mode, leaving move
  mode no longer turns it off.

**Behind the scenes**

- The desktop test head unit can now play held buttons, double presses and hold-and-turn, with
  new bench walks for them.

Host shims: unchanged since 0.1.4 (no need to reinstall).

Nothing in this release has been tried in the car yet.
