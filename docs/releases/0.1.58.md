**Waze's buttons in a wheel, Back twice for home, Car in Settings, and a Preview you can drive**

- **Waze without buttons on the map (ORBIT):** on Waze full screen, its buttons no longer sit on
  the map. Push the knob (or press Back) and they open as a wheel: Waze's actions, then
  "Move map", then "MyPlay home". Turn to choose, push to run it.
- **Back twice for home:** from Waze's map, Back shows the wheel and a second Back straight
  after goes to MyPlay's home. After you turn in the wheel, Back only closes it. A notice
  with buttons still takes Back first (it dismisses the notice). The hints no longer mention
  holding Back.
- **Car data is back on ORBIT:** it is the first row of Settings; Back from it returns to
  Settings.
- **The phone's picture of the car screen shows your colours on ORBIT,** as the car does.
- **Preview (phone: Home › Preview), a testing aid:** the car screen on the phone, driven with the car's own
  knob, pad, Back, Menu and taught buttons, touch, and a pretend speed. Its (i) lists what only
  a car can give. It never teaches a button.
- **Phone apps on a rooted phone:** "Use Shizuku" sits next to "Allow root", and if the root
  prompt is refused the Shizuku steps show instead of staying on Allow root.

It builds and passes its tests, but nothing in it has been tried in the car.

Host shims: unchanged since 0.1.4 (no need to reinstall).
