**Try ORBIT, MyPlay's own car screen (preview)**

- A new switch at the top of the phone's Layout page: "Try ORBIT". The car's home screen then
  becomes ORBIT: Waze's map with four things in its corners. The next turn sits top left, the
  clock, battery and speed top right, the hub bottom left (its ring fills as the trip goes on,
  with the arrival time), and the song bottom right as a round disc with its artwork.
- Turn the knob or use the pad to move the ring between the turn, the hub and the song. A push
  opens the maps app, the dial or the music app.
- Pushing the hub opens a dial of six worlds. Apps opens the usual app grid; the other worlds
  say "Not drawn yet" for now, and Back returns.
- Waze is told what the corners cover, so it keeps its arrow in the free middle.
- Day and night colours. Only the home screen is new: every other screen still looks as before.

**Speed**

- A choice on the phone's Speed page of where the speed comes from: the car, else the phone's
  location (Auto), the car only, or off. The phone's location is used only while the speed is on
  the screen, Waze runs and the car is connected, and only the location Waze already gets: no
  extra battery use.
- The speed limit page: choose the region and download its file on Wi-Fi. No file is published
  yet, so there is nothing to download for now.

**Mirrored phone apps**

- The knob and the pad now reach a mirrored phone app, with a ring on what is chosen. A choice
  per app on the phone of what turning the knob moves. Three quick Backs always go home.

**For the Waze check**

- A "maps app text" check on the phone's Advanced page: it logs only the length of each text
  Waze sends and any speed in it (like "50 km/h"), never the words, roads or addresses.

Host shims: unchanged since 0.1.4 (no need to reinstall).

Nothing in this release has been tried in the car yet.
