**On the phone**

- If the Taskbar's weather card is switched on while you use another layout, the Cards page now
  says so, explains that MyPlay sends a rough position (about 10 km) to Open-Meteo for it, and
  has a button to switch it off.
- The Bar page says that in "Map behind, cards on top" the clock shows only in the "Clock and
  status" card, so keep that card on if you want a clock.
- Two notes that should show quote marks now do.

**On the car screen**

- The status icons and the clock got settings underneath (battery as an icon, a number or both, colours, signal
  shown or not, sizes, and the clock's size, colour and boldness). Nothing looks different yet: the defaults are the same
  look as before, and the phone pages to change them come in a later release.

Nothing in this release has been tried in the car yet.
