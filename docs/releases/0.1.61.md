**Waze's messages in ORBIT's look, its screens in ORBIT's colours by day, moving the map, and estimated speed limits**

- **Waze's messages (ORBIT):** "Arrived" and "Recalculating" show in the turn's place, and Waze's
  message screens use ORBIT's look instead of a plain box.
- **By day,** Waze's screens use ORBIT's day colours.
- **Moving the map (ORBIT):** a crosshair on the middle of the free area of the map and a small
  pill saying "Push or Back to finish", instead of the old line across the top.
- **Speed limits not from a sign look different:** limits your current speed-limit data already
  had from the kind of road or the zone (towns, motorways, living streets), not from a sign on
  the road, are now drawn with a dashed ring and a softer colour, marked "estimate (no sign in
  the map)". This version doesn't fill in any new limits; roads without a limit still show none.
- **Car > Speed > "Fill in standard limits"** (on by default): for now, turning it off hides those
  road-kind and zone limits too, which 0.1.60 showed, so you see only limits from signs.
- **Fixed:** when Waze resent the same message, the banner could flicker and its icon was loaded
  again.

It builds and passes its tests, but nothing in it has been tried in the car.

Host shims: unchanged since 0.1.4 (no need to reinstall).
