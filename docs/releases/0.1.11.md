**On the car screen**

- **Maps on the home screen**, a new choice in the car's own settings:
  - *Last used* (as before): the home screen shows the maps app you used last.
  - *One maps app*: the home screen always shows that one.
  - *Always ask*: the home screen always shows the buttons to pick a maps app.

  The bar's maps button still opens the maps app you used last.
- If a maps app on the home screen shows nothing for 15 seconds (it failed to start, or never
  sent a screen), the home screen goes back to the maps buttons until you open that app yourself.
  The connection log says when this happens.

**On the phone**

- The Backup page keeps the file you chose, and its tile shows the day you last saved.
- A new Size page: car screen text size, control size, and density (Auto or a fixed value).

Nothing in this release has been tried in the car yet.
