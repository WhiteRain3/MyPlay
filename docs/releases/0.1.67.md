**Phone apps on the car: a second way to start them, the exact reason when one won't, and a readable knob hint**

- **A second way to start a phone app:** when the usual way is refused (as can happen with root),
  MyPlay now starts the app on the car screen another way, the same way a shell command would.
  This is aimed at MovieBox Pro not starting on the car. Links from "Play on car" open the same way.
- **The exact reason:** when a phone app still can't start on the car, its message now ends with
  how MyPlay tried (root or Shizuku) and the actual error, for example "su denied", "su gave no
  answer within 8 s", "package not found" or "activity refused display …".
- **Hiding a media player no longer hides the app:** hiding an app's media player on the Apps
  page no longer hides its real app on the car. This is aimed at Morphe missing from the Apps ring.
  So an app hidden on the Apps page but allowed under Phone apps now appears on the car as the
  real app; to remove it, turn it off in Phone apps.
- **The log** now lists, by package name, which phone apps are allowed, hidden or not installed,
  which ones the car shows, and which apps MyPlay considered as the YouTube app. It stays on the
  phone unless you share it; please send it if YouTube or Morphe is still missing.
- **Fixed:** the knob hint's text was white on a pale pill and hard to read; it's dark now.

Please check in the car:
1. MovieBox Pro starts and lands on the car's screen.
2. YouTube (Morphe) is in the Apps ring and opens as the real app, even with its old media entry
   still hidden.
3. If a phone app fails to start as root, its message ends with "(root: …)" and a real reason;
   please send that and the log.
4. If a start takes about 8 seconds and then says "launch timeout", please note it.
5. The knob hint (the small pill telling you what the knob does) is easy to read, by day and by
   night.

It builds and passes its tests, but nothing in it has been tried in the car.

Host shims: unchanged since 0.1.4 (no need to reinstall).
