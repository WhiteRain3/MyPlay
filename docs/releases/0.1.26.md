**On the phone**

- **Map colours**, on the Colours page: a colour filter over the maps app shown on the car screen,
  so its colours come closer to your look. Choose a preset (none, muted, cool, warm, darker
  nights, grey) or set your own: hue, colourfulness, brightness, contrast, a tint, and darker at
  night (which follows the maps app's own night colours). A made-up map shows the map as the app
  draws it and as the car screen will show it.
- It recolours everything the maps app draws on its map, its own buttons and speed sign too, and
  works on colours only (it cannot tell water from roads). It costs the phone a little more work
  per frame, so it is off unless you set one.
- Each look keeps its own map colours: saving a look keeps them, choosing a look brings its own.

Nothing in this release has been tried in the car yet.
