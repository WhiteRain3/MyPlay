**On the phone**

- **A wallpaper editor**: after choosing a photo for the car screen, place it (drag to move,
  pinch to zoom up to three times; it always fills the whole screen), make it darker or blur it,
  and see the result at once. "Use as wallpaper" sends it to the car; "Edit the photo" opens it
  again later, and every edit starts from the untouched photo, so darkening never piles up.
  "Start again" puts the photo back as it came.
- **Updates in one tap**: where MyPlay says a newer version is out, "Download and install"
  fetches it from MyPlay's own release page, checks it (the checksum, that it is MyPlay, that
  version, and signed with the same key as the installed app), then hands it to Android, which
  asks you to confirm. The first time, Android asks you to allow MyPlay to "Install unknown apps";
  allow it, then tap again. On some Android versions this may refuse; it then does nothing
  harmful, and the release page still works as before. Nothing is downloaded without your tap.
- **The Buttons page always leaves a way out**: it no longer offers a choice that would leave no
  button going back or home. The note that a double press makes single presses wait now shows on
  every button that can have one.

**On the car screen**

- On the Taskbar and Dock home screens, the pad on the knob can now reach the music card's
  previous, play and next buttons: press down on the card.

Nothing in this release has been tried in the car yet.
