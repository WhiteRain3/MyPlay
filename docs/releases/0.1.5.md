**On the car screen**

- **Bar items you can choose** (from AARE, by Jason F.): the bar can show the clock, the outside
  temperature (from the car's own sensor when it has one), the phone's battery and the weather.
  Choose which, and in what order, in the car's settings.
- **"Map behind, cards on top"** (the layout added in 0.1.4):
  - On a small screen like the Audi's, the cards now fill their column. Before, they left an
    empty gap where a fourth card would have been.
  - The music card is now one stop for the knob. Press the knob on it to reach its
    previous / play / next buttons; turn on past them to leave the card again. Touching the
    card still opens the music app.

**On the phone**

- The settings now show a small picture of the car screen. Tap a part of it (the bar, the
  clock, a card, the wallpaper) to open the page for that part. There are new pages for the
  colours and the wallpaper.
