**ORBIT stays ORBIT (preview, the "Try MyPlay's own car screen" switch on the phone's Layout page)**

After the first drive: no button leads back to the old layout any more.

- **Apps open inside MyPlay's screen:** the maps app, a music app, any app (the phone's screen and
  the browser too) take the screen beside a narrow strip with a Home button on the driver's side.
  Back still comes home. A notice and an incoming call over an app look like the rest of MyPlay.
- **The knob works over apps:** an incoming call takes the knob while it rings, and afterwards the
  knob is back in the app where it was (a mirrored phone app too). A notice with buttons (Reply,
  Mark read) takes the knob for the few seconds it shows: it starts on Dismiss, so a push meant
  for the app only dismisses it; turn to choose a button, push to use it, Back to dismiss.
- **Built-in apps open MyPlay's own pages:** in Apps, Settings, Car data, Phone and Notifications
  open the matching MyPlay page; Back returns to Apps.
- **No old app grid:** with no maps app chosen, Navigate shows the maps apps to pick from; with no
  music app known, the music library button shows the music apps.
- **Calls:** in a call, the strip on the home opens the Calls page; a call started from another
  app opens it too.

**DECK, the second design (preview):** on the phone's Layout page, under the preview switch,
choose the Design: ORBIT or DECK. DECK's home is a hand of colour-coded cards in the bottom-right
corner (Navigate, Phone, Car, Settings, Apps, Music): turn the knob to swing the hand, push to
open the card in front, Back to come home. The big turn read-out is top left. DECK's pages (music,
apps, calls, car, settings) still use ORBIT's look for now. The switch applies at once.

**Speed limit:** the Car page now has a "Speed limit" line saying why the limit shows or not (for
example "no location fix yet" while parked).

**Still to come:** the line that says what a button just did (next song and so on) does not show
over apps yet.

**Worth trying:** a call while an app is open (then turn the knob: it should still work the app),
a message with Reply arriving while an app is open, and DECK.

Host shims: unchanged since 0.1.4 (no need to reinstall).

Nothing in this release has been tried in the car yet.
