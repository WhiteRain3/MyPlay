**DECK draws its own pages (preview, choose it under the preview switch on the phone's Layout page)**

In 0.1.36 DECK's home was its own but its pages still looked like ORBIT. Now everything in DECK is
DECK's:

- **Each world opens as its card, full screen:** the world's colour with its thick coloured top
  edge and a caps label. Settings, Car, Calls, the notifications list and a music app's library
  are lists of flat rows; the knob's row is outlined in teal.
- **Apps:** a grid of tiles, five a row, two rows a page.
- **Music:** the artwork with the song and its progress (left and right seek there, as in ORBIT),
  a pill of buttons under it (previous, play or pause, next, the library) and the next songs on
  the right.
- **An incoming call:** the green Calls card takes over: Accept, and Decline under it. In a call, a
  small card at the top with the name and the time, and Hang up beside it.
- **A notice, the bell, and the strip beside an app** in DECK's look too.

The knob works the same in DECK and ORBIT; only the look differs.

**The line after a button gesture** (next song, paused, the assistant) now shows over apps too,
and on the home it sits under a notice instead of on top of it.

**Worth trying:** DECK for a drive (switch on the phone's Layout page, it applies at once): an
incoming call, the Apps page with your apps' icons, and the music page.

Host shims: unchanged since 0.1.4 (no need to reinstall).

Nothing in this release has been tried in the car yet.
