**Speed limits (ORBIT, preview)**

The car screen can now show the speed limit under your speed, from OpenStreetMap's map data for
Lithuania. To turn it on, open MyPlay on the phone, go to **Speed**, choose the region
**Lithuania** and download the file (on Wi-Fi, about 3 MB). After that it works offline: the car's
position is never sent anywhere to find a limit.

- The limit is a hint; the signs on the road always win. It is always labelled "limit from map
  data, may be wrong", and it shows "no limit known" whenever MyPlay is not sure which road you
  are on (no road near, a poor GPS fix, two roads side by side with different limits).
- Over the limit by more than a margin, your speed's number turns amber. No sound, nothing
  flashes. The margin is 9 km/h unless you change it (Off, 5, 9 or 15) in the car's Settings
  world or on the phone's Speed page.
- It needs the speed source set to **Auto** (not "Car only"), because the position comes from the
  phone's location. It uses only the location fixes your maps app already asks for, never a GPS
  request of MyPlay's own, so it adds no battery use. As before, only while the speed is on the
  screen, a maps app is running and the car is connected.
- The file is rebuilt from OpenStreetMap and published on MyPlay's own release page; the phone
  looks for a newer one once a month. Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL 1.0.

**Worth trying:** a drive with Waze running: town streets, a road beside a service road, a
motorway, a few junctions. Note where the limit is wrong or says "no limit known".

Host shims: unchanged since 0.1.4 (no need to reinstall).

Nothing in this release has been tried in the car yet.
