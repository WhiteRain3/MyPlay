**Next and previous song from the knob and the wheel, Waze's arrow in ORBIT's middle**

- **Next and previous song without holds** (your car sends no holds): push the knob, or click
  the steering wheel, twice quickly for the next song, three times for the previous one. The
  short line on the screen says which. Because of this, a single push now waits about a quarter
  of a second before it presses. You can switch the double or triple press off on the phone
  (Buttons). While a call rings or is on, or a notice, a Waze alert or the music panel is
  waiting for the knob, a push acts at once (no wait, no double or triple), so it always answers.
- **Waze's arrow:** the map's free area is now chosen better on every layout. With ORBIT, Waze is
  told the tall free middle between ORBIT's corners, not a thin band across the screen, so its
  arrow should sit in the middle.
- **After a Waze alert** the knob's highlight goes back to where it was.
- **The speed limit file:** if the car side cannot read it, the log now says why (instead of
  "not downloaded yet") and it is tried again after 10 seconds.
- **For the next steps, in the connection log:** the knob and button settings the car really
  uses, at each connection and after each change; and, with "maps text" on (Behind the scenes),
  each Waze alert's full shape, so radar, police and average-speed warnings can be designed
  from what Waze really sends. Alert texts are logged in full on the phone while it is on, so
  they can hold street names; it stays off unless you turn it on. A shared Car check log hides
  them unless you tick "Include alert texts" when sharing. Please do one drive with "maps text"
  on and share the log with that box ticked, after reading it.
