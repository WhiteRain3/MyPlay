**ORBIT is complete (preview, the "Try ORBIT" switch on the phone's Layout page)**

All six worlds of the dial now do something:

- **Navigate** opens your maps app full screen (the last one used), with its own search and
  destinations. Back comes home.
- **Music**, **Apps** and **Calls** as in 0.1.32 and 0.1.33.
- **Car:** the car's data to read with the knob: fuel and range, outside temperature, tyres,
  doors and whatever else the car sends.
- **Settings:** what you might change from the car: colours, the maps app on the home screen,
  where the speed comes from, the line after button gestures, message notifications, reading
  messages aloud, phone audio in the car, and passenger mode. A push changes the setting; it
  applies at once.

**Notifications:** a notice now shows at the top of the home for a few seconds, and its buttons
(such as Reply) can be reached with the knob. A bell under the clock shows how many you have not
seen; push it for the list (newest first, Clear all at the end). While driving, messages show who
wrote, not what.

**Passenger mode** (it lifts all driving limits): from the car it can now only be turned on while
the car is stopped, on every layout; it can always be turned off. On the phone nothing changes.

**Worth trying:** a call from a second phone, a few messages arriving during a drive, a music app
with tabs and a long playlist. None of this has met a real call log or music library yet.

Host shims: unchanged since 0.1.4 (no need to reinstall).

Nothing in this release has been tried in the car yet.
