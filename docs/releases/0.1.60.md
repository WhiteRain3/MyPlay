**Go now as a big button, calls and messages in ORBIT's look, the clock on the music ring, and Apps in a ring**

- **"Go now" is a big round button (ORBIT):** on Waze's route preview it sits right of the
  routes, with the knob's highlight already on it: one push starts the drive. Long words shrink
  to fit inside it.
- **Message banners** sit right of the middle, clear of the turn and the clock, with big
  buttons: the first action filled, then a "Dismiss" button. While driving a message shows
  "Message · hidden while driving". The highlight still starts on Dismiss unless you asked for
  the banner (Call back).
- **A ringing call** no longer darkens the screen, so the turn and lanes stay readable: the
  caller's initials in a green ring, Answer (highlighted) and Decline beside it.
- **In a call,** the small call pill sits at the bottom middle, like Waze's alerts, so the turn
  and lanes are clear; while an alert shows there, the pill moves to the top.
- **The time is on the music ring (ORBIT's home):** the outside temperature, the ice sign and the
  battery (only when low or charging) sit beside it; with nothing playing, the empty ring holds
  the time. The status row across the top is gone, so the map's free area no longer moves.
  **The song's title and artist are no longer written beside the ring on the home** (push the
  ring, or open Music, to see them).
- **Apps in a ring:** eight apps a page round a disc; the one the knob is on is larger, with its
  name and "Push to open" in the middle. The pages show as dots.

It builds and passes its tests, but nothing in it has been tried in the car.

Host shims: unchanged since 0.1.4 (no need to reinstall).
