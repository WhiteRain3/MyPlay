**Car buttons**

- **Back, held, goes home a little sooner**: hold Back a little over half a second (0.6 s, it was
  0.7 s) and MyPlay goes home as soon as that time is reached, without waiting for you to let go.
  A short press still goes back.
- Underneath, MyPlay now understands presses, holds, double presses and "hold and turn" on the
  car's buttons, with the hold and double-press times as settings. Only Back uses this so far;
  the other buttons and a phone page to set them come in the next releases.

Nothing in this release has been tried in the car yet.
