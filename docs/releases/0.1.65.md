**YouTube and MovieBox Pro on the car, and Car screenshot on the phone's Home**

- **Video apps on the car as the real apps:** with root or Shizuku, your phone's YouTube app
  (whichever build opens YouTube links, a patched one such as Morphe before Google's) and MovieBox
  Pro run on the car's own screen. The phone's screen is never mirrored. Video plays only when
  parked. Tapping YouTube on the car opens the real app; it isn't listed again as a media player.
- **Which YouTube:** in the phone app's Phone apps, the YouTube section has a "Video app" choice.
  "Automatic" (named with the app MyPlay found) or any app you pick; putting YouTube on or taking
  it off the car then uses that app. Picking a different one doesn't take the previous app off the
  car: remove it in Apps › Phone apps if you want only one.
- **Turned on once:** the first time root or Shizuku is there, MyPlay switches **Phone apps on the
  car** on and adds the YouTube app and MovieBox Pro to it. If you take either off later, it stays
  off.
- **Play on car:** MyPlay is in the phone's share menu. Share a video link from any app and choose
  "Play on car": it opens in the car's video app (only one already allowed on the car, and only
  while connected), and the phone's copy pauses. **While driving the link waits and opens once
  you're parked**, so it may open later than you expect. If you haven't parked within 10 minutes,
  it's dropped and the car says so.
- **Open on car:** if a video app is playing on the phone, its stop on the music panel opens it on
  the car, at the same video when the app tells which one.
- **On the car:** the app fills the screen sideways, and the line along the top fades. Leave it
  (for the map or the home) and come back within 2 minutes and it carries on. Driving off pauses
  it (picture hidden, sound off).
- **Sound:** turn on **Root audio** for the video's sound to reach the car.
- **Car screenshot on the phone's Home:** while the car shows MyPlay, the phone app's Home has a
  "Car screenshot" row in the car's card. A screenshot now waits at most a fifth of a second for
  the car's picture.
