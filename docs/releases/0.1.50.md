**Hints round the speed, Navigate home, and the album art always round**

- **What each control does, at a glance (ORBIT):** small icons on a ring round the speed show
  what the pad's directions open (Waze up, the dial left and right, as you set them on the
  phone) and what a button you taught in the car does (on the upper left). A direction or button
  with nothing to do shows nothing. They appear when you press something, turn the knob or touch
  the screen, and fade after the panel time. They stay out of Waze's area.
- **Navigate home from a button:** teach one of the buttons by the knob "Navigate home" (phone:
  Buttons, Teach a button), and type your home address, or lat,lng, in the new "Home address"
  field under it. The button then starts Waze to that address. Without an address the car says
  "Set home address on the phone". The address is kept in your settings backup like your other
  settings (never in a shared look).
- **Album art is always a circle:** with a long song title the art could be squeezed into an
  oval. It is now always round and cropped, never stretched.
- **New phone app (hidden preview):** More › Updates and What's new, and the Screen pages
  (design, size, wallpaper, map colours). To try the new phone app: Settings → About → tap the
  version line at the bottom 7 times. A countdown shows how many taps are left.

It builds and passes its tests, but nothing in it has been tried in the car.

Host shims: unchanged since 0.1.4 (no need to reinstall).
