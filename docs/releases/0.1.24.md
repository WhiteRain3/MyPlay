**Behind the scenes**

- **For the "map not centred" reports (Waze):** the connection log now records, each time it
  changes, which part of the map MyPlay tells the maps app is free (the map's size and place on
  the screen, and the free area). The Car check's shared log carries these lines, which should
  show why a map sits off-centre or a speed sign is cut at an edge.
- Ready for the phone's settings, not switchable yet: outlining that free area on the car
  screen, and a colour filter for the map (per look), so a maps app's colours can be brought
  closer to the chosen look.
- The song's artwork is prepared once per picture instead of on every screen update.

Nothing in this release has been tried in the car yet.
