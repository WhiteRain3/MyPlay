**Car buttons** (from AARE, by Jason F.)

- Buttons on the steering wheel and dash are passed on more fully: a button held down now counts
  as a long press even on cars that never say so themselves, and the shift state of a key
  reaches the screen.
- Cars whose touchpad is meant for moving around the screen can now move the ring with it, and
  get the car's own click feedback.
- Underneath, a table for giving the car's buttons other actions. The phone page to change them
  comes later; until then every button does what it did before.
- The Back button keeps MyPlay's own timing: pressed, it goes back; held for about 0.7 seconds,
  it goes home. A Back held only a little long, or a car slow to report the release, no longer
  sends you home by mistake.

**On the car screen**

- After switching to a layout that shows the weather again, the weather appears within a few
  minutes instead of up to half an hour later.

Nothing in this release has been tried in the car yet.
