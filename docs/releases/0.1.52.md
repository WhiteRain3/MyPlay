**Remember where I parked, a limit that pulses, ORBIT polish**

- **Remember where I parked:** when the car disconnects, MyPlay can keep the spot where you
  parked, on the phone only (never in the log, never in a backup), and give you a way back:
  walking directions in Google Maps, or in any maps app. Off by default; turn it on on the phone.
- **The speed limit sign pulses once** when the limit changes, so a new limit catches the eye.
- **The outside temperature under ORBIT's clock** (for example "3°"), when the car reports it.
- **Ice and phone warnings are on by default** (since 0.1.51, whose notes left that out): the
  snowflake at 4 °C or less, the lines about ice and a hot phone, and now also the limit's pulse
  and the temperature under the clock. Phone: Layout, "Ice and phone warnings", turns them all
  off.
- **ORBIT polish:** the battery number keeps one width, so the clock's column never shifts; the
  short line at the top uses ORBIT's own glass; a notice with buttons on the home now takes the
  knob's highlight, on Dismiss (on Call for Call back), as Waze's alerts do; the limit sign is a softer light grey inside at night.
- **New phone app (hidden preview):** the Apps pages, and Places beside the home address. To try
  it: Settings → About → tap the version line at the bottom 7 times.

It builds and passes its tests, but nothing in it has been tried in the car.

Host shims: unchanged since 0.1.4 (no need to reinstall).
