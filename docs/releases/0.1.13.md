**On the phone**

- **What's new after an update**: the first time MyPlay opens after an update, it shows that
  version's notes, like these. Close them with "Got it" or Back. About can show them again.
  The notes come with the app; nothing is downloaded.
- **Drag cards into place** for "Map behind, cards on top" (Layout and Cards pages): drag a card
  onto a place to put it there, or onto the tray below to hide it. The arrows still work too.
- **The Bar page for every layout that has bar items**, now including the Dock and "Map
  behind". Its notes say what the bar shows in your layout, and when the "Clock and status"
  card does not fit your car's screen (on the Audi's, only three cards fit), it says to move
  that card up on the Cards page.
- A maps app chosen for the home screen that is hidden or removed makes the home screen show
  the maps buttons; the Cards page says so.

**On the car screen**

- The music card: after turning back onto it from its buttons, pressing the knob goes into
  the buttons again (it did nothing before).
- The weather is only fetched when the layout you use shows it somewhere, so fewer requests go
  out.

Nothing in this release has been tried in the car yet.
