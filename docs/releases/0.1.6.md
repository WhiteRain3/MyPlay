**On the phone**

- MyPlay's window is dark from the very first moment it opens, instead of flashing white first.
- The Bar and Cards pages now show the options of the car screen layout you are using, so you
  only see what applies to your layout.
- Google's Android Auto no longer shows up in the Apps page and its tile: MyPlay cannot show
  its screens anyway, and the car's app grid already leaves it out.
- The Layout page got a few small fixes and clearer wording.
- On the pages for each part of the car picture, you can now scroll even when your finger
  starts on the picture.

Nothing in this release has been tried in the car yet.
