**Car buttons: what each one does now**

Until the phone has a page to change them, these are the defaults. Which button is which on your
car is a best guess until MyPlay has seen your car's connection log.

- **Knob, pressed**: presses what has the ring, as before, but now when you **let go** of the knob,
  not when you push it down.
- **Knob, held about 0.6 s**: **next song**. Careful if you rest a finger on the knob.
- **Knob, held and turned**: one song on or back per click, if your car sends the turns while the
  knob is held (not known yet).
- **Play/pause on the steering wheel**: still play/pause, but it waits about a quarter of a second
  first, in case a second click follows: **two quick clicks** go to the **previous song**; **held**,
  it goes to the **next song**.
- **Menu**: goes **home**; on the home screen, opens the **app grid**. **Held**, it opens the
  **voice assistant**. Before, it did nothing.
- **Back**: as in 0.1.15: pressed, back; held a little over half a second, home.

Nothing in this release has been tried in the car yet.
