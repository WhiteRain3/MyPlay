**Videos in the car browser go fullscreen, and YouTube on the car screen (parked)**

- **Fullscreen video:** a video's fullscreen button in the car browser now fills the whole car
  screen. Back leaves fullscreen.
- **YouTube:** a YouTube entry next to the browser in the app list (the browser must be on, on the
  phone: Settings for the car screen). It opens YouTube's mobile site in the car browser; its
  videos can go fullscreen. Like the browser, it is paused while driving.

This release went out quickly at your request: it builds and passes its tests, but it has not
been reviewed yet and nothing in it has been tried in the car. If a video still does not go
fullscreen, say on which site.

Host shims: unchanged since 0.1.4 (no need to reinstall).
