**ORBIT: no side strip, the phone screen fills, a music panel**

- **No more strip with the Home button beside apps (ORBIT).** An app, the phone screen and the
  browser take the whole car screen. Use the car's Home button (Menu), or hold Back, to come home.
  Back from an app goes back to where you opened it (the Apps page on that app, or the home), and
  three quick Backs leave any app, even the phone screen (also when the app would
  use those Backs itself, such as going back through the browser's pages). "Hold Back to come home" shows the
  first three times in a drive. Apps grow out of what you picked and shrink back on Back.
- **The phone screen fills the car screen** when the phone is sideways: it is stretched to the
  whole screen instead of having black bars. Upright, it keeps its shape (stretched it would be
  unreadable). After you turn the phone, it takes about a second to follow.
- **The music disc opens controls, not the app.** Pushing the song on ORBIT's home opens a small
  panel above it: previous, play/pause, next, and the progress bar (left and right skip 10 s
  there). The music app is one button in the panel. Back closes it.
- **Panels close by themselves.** The music panel and the dial of worlds close after 5 s without
  the knob, pad or a touch. Change the time, or turn it off, on the phone (Layout page, under the
  design choice) or in the car's Settings ("Close panels by themselves": 3, 5, 8 or 15 s, or never).
- **The car's Home button on MyPlay's screen** (in the Apps page, say) now goes straight home.
- **Knob push codes:** the number pad's Enter now counts as the knob's push, and a push-like key
  MyPlay does not know is written to the connection log once.

**Please try this in the car:** run the Car check on the phone, and press the knob, the pad, and
each of the 4 buttons around the knob. Then share the log. It shows which code each one sends, so
the knob's push can be rebound properly and those 4 buttons can be given actions (Waze, music,
apps…).

It builds and passes its tests, but nothing in it has been tried in the car. DECK is unchanged
(it keeps its strip).

Host shims: unchanged since 0.1.4 (no need to reinstall).
