**On the car screen**

- **The bar items you chose** (clock, outside temperature, phone battery, weather) now also show
  on the Dock's bar and on the bar of "Map behind, cards on top", not only on the taskbar.
  In "Map behind", while the "Clock and status" card is on the screen, the bar leaves out what
  that card already shows, so nothing appears twice. On a small screen like the Audi's, where
  that card does not fit, the bar keeps the clock.
- A temperature item you chose but that has no reading (no sensor in the car and the weather
  off) shows a dimmed "–°" instead of nothing. The weather item does the same while the weather
  is on but not fetched yet; with the weather off it shows nothing.
- **"Map behind" with no maps app on the home screen** now offers "Where to?" and the maps
  buttons in the free space, instead of only the wallpaper.
- A maps app that was slow to start and was left off the home screen comes back by itself as
  soon as it shows its screen (within 5 minutes).
- At the larger text and control sizes, the side bar gives up its extra items sooner on short
  screens, so it still fits.

**On the phone**

- The Cards page lets you choose which map the home screen shows (last used, always ask, or
  one maps app).
- On the Size page, a density the car would ignore shows as Auto.

Nothing in this release has been tried in the car yet.
