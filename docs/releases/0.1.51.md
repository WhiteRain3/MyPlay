**Ice and phone warnings, Places, Call back**

- **Ice and phone warnings (ORBIT):** if the car reports the outside temperature, a snowflake
  shows by the clock at 4 °C or less, with a short line once per drive ("the road may be icy").
  A short line also says when the phone gets hot enough to slow the screen. Phone: Layout, "Ice
  and phone warnings". The clock's status row now always keeps its place, so Waze's free area
  shifts once after this update and then stays put.
- **Places:** a home pad direction can open a list of Home and up to five saved places (phone:
  Buttons, next to the home address). Turn or press up and down to choose, push to go. Off until
  you set a direction to Places.
- **Call back the last missed call:** an action for a taught button or the home pad. It never
  dials by itself: it asks "Call back …?" and only a push on Call places the call. Off until you
  give it to a button or a direction.
- **Drive summary:** one line in the connection log when a drive ends (how long, how far, how
  often the speed and its limit were known), to see how well the speed limit file covers your
  roads. It holds each drive's minutes and kilometres (no places, no speeds), and the connection
  log is what you share from the Car check.
- **Home address no longer in backups:** the home address, and the new places, now stay on this
  phone and are not saved in the settings backup file (0.1.50's notes said the address was). A
  backup saved with 0.1.50 still has the home address in it.
- **New phone app (hidden preview):** the Car pages (Buttons, pad shortcuts, timing, teaching,
  maps, speed, sound, messages and calls, voice) and the Screen colours and looks. To try the new
  phone app: Settings → About → tap the version line at the bottom 7 times.

It builds and passes its tests, but nothing in it has been tried in the car.

Host shims: unchanged since 0.1.4 (no need to reinstall).
