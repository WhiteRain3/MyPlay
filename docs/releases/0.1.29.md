**With the knob**

- **A ringing call:** the ring goes to Answer, so a push of the knob answers; Back declines the
  call wherever the ring is (Android 9 and later).
- **Music:** with the ring on the now-playing progress bar, left and right on the pad jump 10
  seconds back or on.
- **Maps apps on the car screen** (Organic Maps, Waze and others MyPlay hosts): the map itself
  is now the knob's last stop, after the app's buttons. Push it to move the map: the pad moves
  it, turning zooms in or out, and a push or Back stops. A line at the bottom says so meanwhile.
  Touch works as before.

Nothing in this release has been tried in the car yet, and moving the map has not been tried
with a real maps app.
