**YouTube by the knob, and fullscreen video fixed for driving off**

- **YouTube** now opens YouTube's TV interface, the one made for a remote control: you can see
  where the knob is pointing, and the video's controls (fullscreen, sound, captions) come up when
  you press a button instead of disappearing. If YouTube does not give the TV interface, you get
  its ordinary site, now with the outline below.
- **Every web page in the car browser** shows where the knob is pointing with a bright outline
  (with a thin dark edge round it, so it shows on light and dark pages). Pages look a little
  different because of it.
- **YouTube in the phone's app list:** it can be moved and hidden like the other apps (phone:
  car screen apps).
- **Fullscreen video and driving off:** starting to drive while a video was fullscreen used to
  hide the Home button for the whole drive and leave a black screen after parking. Fixed.

It builds and passes its tests, but the YouTube TV interface could not be tried before release
(YouTube is not reachable from where it is built), and nothing in it has been tried in the car.
Worth checking: does YouTube open its TV interface; do turning and pushing the knob move and
choose; does the car's Back key leave a video (it goes back one page); does fullscreen work; and
does starting to drive with a video fullscreen bring the screen back. If YouTube shows its
ordinary site, or the knob does not move or show there, say so.

Host shims: unchanged since 0.1.4 (no need to reinstall).
