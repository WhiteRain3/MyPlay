**Phone apps on the car: never a mirror of the phone, and a plain message when one can't start**

- **Never a mirror:** when a phone app (YouTube, MovieBox Pro) refuses to run on the car's screen,
  the car no longer ends up showing the phone's screen, which looked like mirroring. It shows a
  plain message instead, saying why: MyPlay has no root or Shizuku; the phone refused the car
  screen; the app wouldn't start; or the app opened on the phone because it refuses other screens.
- **Rooted phones:** a rooted phone now lists its phone apps on the car even before Root audio is
  set up, and the car tries them. This should fix YouTube missing from the car. If root is then
  refused, the car says so, and the phone's Phone apps page shows the root or Shizuku card.
- **Video app swap (correction to 0.1.65's notes):** choosing a different "Video app" now swaps it
  on the car. The new one goes on and the previous one comes off (if it was there), even if you
  leave the page straight away. 0.1.65's notes said the previous app stayed; that's no longer so.
- **Preview** counts as parked unless its pretend speed is above 0.
