**On the phone**

- **Car check**, a new tile on the start screen. With the phone connected to the car, it walks
  you through the car's controls one at a time: turn the knob both ways, push and hold it, push
  the pad up, down, left and right, Menu, Back, hold Back, and the wheel's scroll, click and
  hold. For each step it shows what the car sent as it arrives, and ticks the step off. You can
  go back to any step, or skip one when nothing happens.
- "Share the log" sends what the check found, what the car said about its screen and its
  controls, and the connection log, through Android's share sheet. Read it before sending: it
  is meant to help find out which codes your car's buttons send.
- The shared log leaves out private details: the names (or numbers) of people who got an
  automatic reply, Bluetooth device names, the car's Wi-Fi network name, phone numbers, and all
  but the last part of Bluetooth addresses.

**Behind the scenes**

- The connection log itself no longer writes down those names in the first place.

Nothing in this release has been tried in the car yet.
