**ORBIT: Waze's alerts in their own place, softer shading, the speed limit file kept**

- **Waze's alerts** ("In 380 m · Reduced speed · Close") are now ORBIT's own compact pill in the
  map's free lower middle, above the hub and the song, never over the next turn. When one comes,
  the knob's highlight goes to its button (usually Close), so a push answers the alert. The wide
  grey card at the top is gone on ORBIT's home.
- **A better shade:** instead of the two wide dark corners, a soft, blurred plate hugs each part
  that floats on the map (the turn, the song, the clock), dark at night and light by day; the
  map's middle stays clear. Choose "Soft" or "None" on the phone's Layout page or in the car's
  Settings ("Shade behind ORBIT's parts").
- **The speed limit file:** choosing "None" as the region on the phone no longer deletes the
  downloaded file, and every delete is now written in the log with its reason. The connection log
  also says, once per drive, what the car side sees of the file. The file may now also download
  on mobile data if you allow it (never while roaming).
- **Privacy:** with ORBIT on screen (which shows no weather), the phone no longer sends its
  approximate position to the weather service.

It builds and passes its tests, but nothing in it has been tried in the car.

Host shims: unchanged since 0.1.4 (no need to reinstall).
