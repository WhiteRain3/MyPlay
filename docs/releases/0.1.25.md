**On the phone**

- **"Show the map's free area"** (Advanced): with it on, the car screen outlines the part of the
  map that MyPlay tells the maps app is free: green for now, dashed yellow for always. Use it to
  see why a map such as Waze is not centred, or why its speed sign is cut at an edge, and turn it
  off when done. It is not saved in backups.

**Behind the scenes**

- The map colour filter (not switchable yet) is now kept with each look, and its "darker at
  night" follows the maps' own day and night.

Nothing in this release has been tried in the car yet.
