**ORBIT (preview, the "Try ORBIT" switch on the phone's Layout page)**

- **Music library:** in the Music world, the library button now opens the music app's folders
  and songs in ORBIT's own style, with their artwork. Turn the knob or use the pad's up and down
  to choose, push to open a folder or play a song (then you are back on now playing), Back to go
  up a folder and then out.
- **Calls:** an incoming call fills the screen with the caller's initials, the name, and Answer
  and Decline (Decline only when MyPlay is allowed to end calls). The ring starts on Answer, so a
  push answers; Back declines as before. While a call is on, a strip at the top of the home shows
  the caller and the time; the knob reaches it (it opens the phone) and Hang up beside it.
  Afterwards the ring is back where it was.
- **The Calls world:** your recent calls (missed ones in red), then favourite contacts, then
  "Keypad and all contacts". One push calls, as on the old phone screen.

**Not yet tried with real apps:** the library and the Calls world have only been tested with
stand-ins, not yet with a real music app or the phone's real call log. Worth trying: a music app
with tabs and a long playlist, and a call from a second phone.

Host shims: unchanged since 0.1.4 (no need to reinstall).

Nothing in this release has been tried in the car yet.
