**The new phone app: setup, search and polish**

- **New phone app (hidden preview):** a first-run setup and a search across all its settings;
  polish for large screens and large text; holding the screen picture names its parts; the
  Looks row says when a look was changed; the pad shortcuts drawn round.
- **Stop a speed limit file download** (new phone app, Car › Speed): the part fetched is thrown
  away and the file you had stays. After Stop it is not fetched again by itself until you press
  Download or next month's look comes round.
- To try the new phone app: Settings → About → tap the version line at the bottom 7 times.
