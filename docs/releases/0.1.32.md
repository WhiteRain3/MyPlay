**ORBIT (preview, the "Try ORBIT" switch on the phone's Layout page)**

- **Fix:** the song's ring on the home screen showed full while a song played. It now shows how
  far into the song you are.
- **Apps:** choosing Apps on the dial now opens ORBIT's own page: your apps as round discs, ten to
  a page. Turn the knob or use the pad to move between them, push or tap to open one, Back to
  return to the dial.
- **Music:** choosing Music on the dial opens the song's artwork with previous, play/pause and
  next under it, and the next songs beside it. With the ring on the artwork, left and right on
  the pad skip 10 seconds back or forward (when the music app allows it). Pushing a next song
  plays it; the library button opens the music app's own screen.
- **On the home screen:** pad right moves the ring towards the song; with the ring on the song,
  pad right plays the next song. A tap on the turn, the hub or the song does what a push does.
- On the phone, tapping a part of ORBIT in the car picture opens its settings page.

Host shims: unchanged since 0.1.4 (no need to reinstall).

Nothing in this release has been tried in the car yet.
