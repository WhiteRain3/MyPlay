**The limit pulse's own switch, steadier numbers, ORBIT fixes**

- **The speed limit pulse has its own switch:** "Limit sign pulse" (phone: Layout, and the car's
  Settings), on by default. 0.1.52's notes said "Ice and phone warnings" also turns it off; it
  no longer does.
- **A notice and a Waze alert hand the knob back properly:** after either one goes, the knob's
  highlight returns to where it was, whichever came first.
- **Numbers keep still:** the clock and other ticking numbers keep a fixed width, so nothing
  beside them shifts (and Waze's free area never moves on the minute); a call past an hour reads
  1:05:03.
- **Long Waze alert buttons** are cut with "…", so the alert's title keeps its room.
- **New phone app (hidden preview):** the More pages (Cars, a car's page, Connection, Backup,
  Diagnostics), and Remember where I parked under Car › Maps. To try it: Settings → About → tap
  the version line at the bottom 7 times.

It builds and passes its tests, but nothing in it has been tried in the car.

Host shims: unchanged since 0.1.4 (no need to reinstall).
